# SupplyChain360 — Business Requirements

## 1. Business Problem

A wholesale distribution company wants to reduce stockouts, backorders,
supplier-driven delays and excessive inventory while improving order
fulfillment and supplier reliability.

The goal of SupplyChain360 is to provide management with a single
decision-support platform for monitoring inventory, procurement,
supplier performance, order fulfillment and demand.

## 2. Primary Stakeholders

- Chief Operating Officer (COO)
- Supply Chain Manager
- Procurement Manager
- Inventory Manager
- Warehouse Manager
- Sales Operations Manager

## 3. Key Business Questions

1. Which products are currently at risk of stockout?
2. Which products are overstocked or slow-moving?
3. Which suppliers are causing procurement delays?
4. Where are backorders increasing?
5. Which products contribute most to demand?
6. Which suppliers provide the best combination of cost and reliability?
7. How efficiently is inventory being utilized?
8. Which products require immediate replenishment?
9. Where are fulfillment bottlenecks occurring?
10. How is supply-chain performance changing over time?

## 4. Business Objectives

### Reduce
- Stockouts
- Backorders
- Excess inventory
- Supplier delays
- Fulfillment delays

### Improve
- Inventory turnover
- Supplier reliability
- Order fulfillment
- Procurement efficiency
- Demand visibility

## 5. Analytics Areas

### Inventory Analytics
- Stock on hand
- Stock value
- Inventory turnover
- Low-stock products
- Overstocked products
- Slow-moving products

### Procurement Analytics
- Purchase quantity
- Purchase cost
- Supplier activity
- Procurement trends
- Lead-time performance

### Supplier Analytics
- Supplier ranking
- Purchase volume
- Delivery reliability
- Lead-time consistency
- Supplier performance score

### Order Fulfillment
- Total orders
- Ordered quantity
- Picked quantity
- Backorders
- Fill rate
- Demand trends

### Sales & Demand
- Revenue
- Quantity sold
- Profit
- Product demand
- Customer demand
- Geographic demand

## 6. Success Criteria

SupplyChain360 should enable stakeholders to:

- Detect inventory risks quickly
- Identify supplier-related bottlenecks
- Prioritize replenishment decisions
- Monitor procurement and fulfillment performance
- Compare suppliers and products
- Perform root-cause analysis
- Make decisions from one governed BI platform

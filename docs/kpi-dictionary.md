# SupplyChain360 — KPI Dictionary

## Inventory KPIs

### Total Inventory Value
Total monetary value of inventory currently held.

Formula:
Quantity On Hand × Last Cost Price

### Stockout SKUs
Number of stock items where Quantity On Hand <= 0.

### Reorder Required SKUs
Number of stock items where Quantity On Hand <= Reorder Level.

### Overstock SKUs
Number of stock items where Quantity On Hand > Target Stock Level.

### Healthy Inventory %
Percentage of stock items classified as Healthy.

---

## Procurement KPIs

### Total Purchase Orders
Distinct number of supplier purchase orders.

### Ordered Quantity
Total quantity ordered from suppliers.

### Received Quantity
Total quantity received from suppliers.

### Outstanding Quantity
Ordered Quantity - Received Quantity.

### Procurement Fulfillment %
Received Quantity / Ordered Quantity × 100.

---

## Order Fulfillment KPIs

### Total Orders
Distinct customer orders.

### Backorder Rate %
Backordered order lines / Total order lines × 100.

### Pending Pick Lines
Order lines that have not yet been picked.

### Average Days to Pick
Average number of days between Order Date and Picked Date.

---

## Sales & Demand KPIs

### Revenue
Total sales value excluding tax.

### Total Profit
Total realized profit.

### Profit Margin %
Profit / Revenue × 100.

### Units Sold
Total quantity sold.

### Average Delivery Days
Average days between Invoice Date and Delivery Date.

---

## Stock Movement KPIs

### Total Stock In
Total positive inventory movement.

### Total Stock Out
Absolute quantity of negative inventory movement.

### Net Stock Movement
Stock In - Stock Out.
